## 6. SHOWDOWNS

### 6.1 Two Types of Showdowns
Spektt hosts two types of creative competitions:

- **Pulse Showdowns** — 100% fan-voted. Community votes determine rankings. Prizes are XP bonuses and exclusive Showdown placement badges (gold, silver, bronze). No cash prizes.
- **Premium Showdowns** — Two-stage judging. Fan votes determine the top 20 finalists. A panel of expert judges scores those finalists. Final rankings combine fan votes (50%) and judge scores (50%). Winners receive cash prizes paid to a Nigerian bank account.

### 6.2 Showdown Eligibility
To enter any Showdown you must:
- Be 18 or older (minors cannot enter Showdowns of any type)
- Not be located in Qatar, UAE, or Saudi Arabia
- Not be assigned as a judge for that Showdown

For Premium Showdowns (cash prizes), you must additionally:
- Be a Nigerian bank account holder (prize payments are currently available to Nigerian participants only)

### 6.3 Entry Rules
- One entry per user per Showdown
- You may submit a new upload or select an existing post from your profile
- Your entry must be original work that you own and that fits the Showdown brief
- Entries may not be withdrawn once the voting stage begins

### 6.4 Entry Display and Randomisation
Entries are displayed in a randomised order that is unique to each viewer. The order is determined by a combination of your user ID and the Showdown ID — this means you will always see the same order yourself, but it differs for every other user. This randomisation is intentional and designed to ensure all entries receive fair visibility regardless of the creator's follower count. It is not a malfunction.

### 6.5 Voting
- Each registered Spektt user may cast one vote per Showdown
- You may vote in multiple Showdowns, up to a daily cap of 20 votes across all Showdowns
- Vote counts are hidden until the Showdown ends — no user can see how many votes any entry has received during the voting period
- Vote manipulation (multiple accounts, coordinated voting, purchasing votes) is a violation of these Terms and results in immediate disqualification and account action

### 6.6 Winner Determination

**Pulse Showdowns**: Entries are ranked by vote count (highest first). Ties are broken by views (higher views ranks higher), then by submission time (earlier submission ranks higher). Top 15 entries are displayed.

**Premium Showdowns**: A panel of judges (maximum 7, selected by the Showdown host) scores each of the top 20 fan-voted entries on defined criteria (0–10 per criterion). Final Score = (Normalised Vote Score × 50%) + (Average Judge Score × 50%). Top 15 entries are displayed.

### 6.7 Judging Decisions
Judge scores and decisions are final. Spektt does not interfere with individual judge scoring. If you believe there was a genuine rules violation (vote manipulation, plagiarised entry, ineligible participant), you may report it via the in-app report function or by emailing showdowns@spektt.com. Disagreement with scores or the community's votes is not grounds for a dispute. Reports of genuine violations will be reviewed and may result in disqualification and re-ranking.

### 6.8 Prizes — Pulse Showdowns
Pulse Showdown prizes are XP bonuses and placement badges (gold, silver, bronze). These have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash. Spektt reserves the right to adjust XP values and badge designs at any time.

### 6.9 Prizes — Premium Showdowns (Cash)
Cash prizes are awarded to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place. Prize amounts are shown on the Showdown detail page.

**Claiming your prize:**
- You will be notified when results are announced
- You have **14 days** from the announcement to submit your claim
- To claim, you must provide your bank name, bank account number, and account name. A Tax ID is optional but recommended
- A **10% withholding tax** is deducted from all cash prizes in accordance with Nigerian tax law before payment is made
- Prizes are paid to Nigerian bank accounts only (10-digit NUBAN format)
- If you do not claim within 14 days, your prize is **permanently forfeited** — no exceptions
- Spektt is not responsible for payment failures caused by inaccurate bank details provided by the winner

### 6.10 Insufficient Entries
If a Showdown does not receive the minimum number of entries (5 for Pulse, 10 for Premium) by the close of the submission window, the Showdown will close without a result. No prizes will be awarded.

### 6.11 Cancelled Showdowns
Spektt reserves the right to cancel any Showdown at any time due to insufficient participation, rule violations, technical issues, or other reasons. In the event of cancellation, no prizes are awarded and entries are not eligible for compensation.
